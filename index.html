A SIDEWAYS APPROACH

The thinking behind the cards.

Dolphin Logic is a creative instrument drawn from the Journal Maison-Page: story seeds, images, musical devices, questions, quotations and ways of living.

Choose a medium. Draw one card from each of its five decks. Hold a useful card, swap another, or deliberately choose one from the collection. The brief brings the instructions together; you decide how to connect them.

What the numbers mean

The bank has 225 source motifs, arranged in five families of 45. Each motif has an English rendering for Story, Image and Music: 675 medium-specific cards in all. These are three renderings of the source material, not 675 independent ideas from the journal.

Story: Figure · Tension · Place · Method · Perspective.
Image: Presence · Relationship · Atmosphere · Treatment · Reading.
Music: Voice · Motion · Space · Technique · Form.

Five independent choices give 45⁵ = 184,528,125 possible draws per medium. A combination is a starting point, not a guarantee of a good result.

The idea and its wording

Every card opens onto three layers: the English adaptation for the selected medium, a conservative English account of the journal idea, and the original French passage. Ellipses mark omissions in the passages.

The English may clarify, rephrase or transpose an idea to another medium. It does not claim that those English sentences occur in the journal. The source idea stays available for comparison. Quotations encountered in the journal retain their identified attribution.

The tarot interpretations from the draft have been removed. The journal's suggestion of combining a theme and a tarot card remains part of the history of the method; this edition's cards are grounded in journal passages.

Where the dolphin comes from

The journal returns to a framework attributed there to La Stratégie du Dauphin: withdraw, change the situation, change your response, or endure for a while. Here, the dolphin stands for finding another way through.

A nod to Eno and Schmidt

The sideways approach acknowledges Brian Eno and Peter Schmidt’s Oblique Strategies. The journal explicitly asks about the right dose of constraint and play. The English renderings here were prepared for Dolphin Logic. Visit Brian Eno’s official shop ↗
